About Us

Welcome to Paws and Purpose.

Our Story

Paws & Purpose is a non-profit organisation founded in 2019 in Cape Town. What began as a small group of volunteer dog-walkers noticing how many local seniors felt isolated has since grown into a full animal rescue and rehoming service. We take in abandoned and surrendered animals, care for them, and help them find loving new homes. Alongside our rescue work, we run a pet therapy programme that pairs our rescued animals with elderly residents in care homes, bringing comfort, companionship, and a little joy to those who need it most.

Our Mission and Vision

Our mission is to rescue and rehome animals in need, giving them a second chance at a loving life. Our vision is to create a world where every animal has the opportunity to live a happy, healthy life in a safe and loving environment.

Meet Max and Grace

Max was found wandering the streets, thin, frightened, and alone. After weeks of care and patience from our team, he blossomed into a gentle, affectionate dog ready for a new chapter. Today, Max visits Sunnyside Care Home every week as part of our pet therapy programme, where he has formed a special bond with 84-year-old resident Grace. "Max gives me something to look forward to every week," Grace says. "He's brought so much joy back into my life." Stories like Max and Grace's are exactly why we do what we do.
