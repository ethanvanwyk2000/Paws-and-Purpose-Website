About the Therapy Programme

Our therapy programme is designed to provide emotional support and companionship to individuals in need. Our trained therapy animals visit hospitals, nursing homes, and schools to bring comfort and joy to those they meet.

Benefits for Seniors

Our therapy programme offers numerous benefits for seniors, including reduced stress, improved mood, and increased social interaction. The presence of therapy animals can also help to alleviate feelings of loneliness and isolation.

Benefits for pets

Our therapy programme also provides benefits for pets, including opportunities for socialization, mental stimulation, and physical exercise. Therapy animals can help to reduce anxiety and stress in pets, and can also improve their overall well-being.

Max and Grace

Max was found wandering the streets, thin and frightened. Today, he visits Sunnyside Care Home every week, where he has formed a special bond with 84-year-old resident Grace. "Max gives me something to look forward to every week," Grace says. "He's brought so much joy back into my life."
